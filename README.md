# Students Social Media Addiction – Power BI Dashboard

## Overview
A 6-page interactive Power BI report analyzing how social media use relates to students' academic performance, sleep, mental health and relationships. The dataset covers **705 students** from multiple countries and academic levels.

## Dashboard Pages
1. **Executive Overview**: KPI cards, addiction score by academic level, gender split, usage hours by age
2. **Mental Health & Lifestyle**: addiction score vs. mental health, sleep hours by age, country table, gender and age slicers
3. **Academic Impact**
4. **Relationships & Conflicts**: relationship status and conflict level, country and academic-level filters
5. **Interactive Story View**: bookmark buttons to switch between Academic and Gender views
6. **Student Profile**: drill-through page with student-level details

## Key Insights
- The dataset has 705 students, split almost evenly by gender (353 female, 352 male).
- Students spend **4.9 hours** a day on social media on average and sleep **6.87 hours** a night.
- **64.3%** of students say social media affects their academic performance.
- Undergraduates make up about half the sample (50.07%), graduates 46.1% and high school students 3.83%.
- Average usage is highest at age 18 (5.4 hrs), falls to its lowest at age 23 (4.5 hrs), then rises again at 24.

## Tools & Techniques
- Power BI Desktop
- Data modeling with a one-to-one relationship on `Student_ID`
- DAX calculated columns, measures and time intelligence (DateTable)
- Slicers, bookmarks, page navigation buttons and drill-through
